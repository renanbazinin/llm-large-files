# Eval baseline: 0.9.0

The baseline of the evals for 0.10.0: 51 questions in 43 chats (`evals/cases.ts`), on Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Gemini 3.8 Flash over the Gemini API, three runs of each question (pass^3). The request bodies are those of 0.9.0 (package A of 0.10.0 changes no request byte; test/request-bodies.test.ts says so). Every later prompt change is compared with this.

- Run 1 of each model is the recording in `evals/cassettes`; runs 2 and 3 were live (`npm run eval:live -- --k 2`). They were run again on 2026-11-24, after facts that let a wrong answer pass or failed a right one were fixed, and after the two whole-read cases were added. Each answer is kept in `0.9.0.json`, so a fact fixed later is checked on it again.
- Input tokens: 6,683,653 for the three runs of both models (Flash-Lite 3,623,075, Flash 3,060,578). Runs 2 and 3 used 4,319,460, and trying and recording the whole-read cases 1,473,137. With the first baseline (about 8.1 million), the live runs used about 13.9 million. The cap was 25 million.
- "Cases passed in every run" is pass^3. A case passes when every fact of every question holds. "Cached share" is the provider's cached input tokens over all input tokens.
- The input-token ceiling was never reached: 0.9.0 has none by default.
- Citations are "not checked yet": `checkCitations` is not written in this version, so the `mustCite` facts and the citation gates wait for it.
- Whole reads: contract-summary and diary-timeline read the whole file in every run (parts, then a final answer; the contract's notes merged too), except diary-timeline on Flash, which finds the five incidents with searches and reads and is not asked to read whole. These are the recorded runs of T's part prompts (M8, M9).
- Findings on 0.9.0, each case marked `failsOn090` with its reason below: Flash-Lite follows the instruction planted in the policy, which goes to the model whole, in 1 to 3 of 3 runs of every question over it, more often than in the first baseline; tables sent whole are counted, summed and looked up by eye, with no tools; and Flash-Lite answers the English questions over Hebrew files in Hebrew (policy-travel-en and budget-sum-en in 3 of 3 runs, Flash budget-sum-en in 2 of 3), which no fact checks.
- The files are synthetic (`evals/fixtures.ts`).
